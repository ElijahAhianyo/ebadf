Hi there! Welcome to War Stories. I wanted to get into the habit of writing down some of the things I learn while working on software as blogs, but I got into a bad habit of spending too much time perfecting the content and getting lost in rabbit holes of research, leaving so many draft posts that never see the light of day. So I decided to create a new branch of writing that is more like a field notebook, where I can write down short notes from real debugging sessions, production lessons, design tradeoffs, and the strange little decisions that shape software. I call this series War Stories, a term I stole from the Algorithm Design Manual by Steven Skiena, which is a great book that I highly recommend.

I hope you enjoy reading them as much as I enjoy writing them.
